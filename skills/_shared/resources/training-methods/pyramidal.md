# Method — Pyramidal Training

Sometimes called **"threshold-pyramidal"** or **"tempo-rich"** intensity distribution.

## Origin

Not attributable to a single coach — it's the historical norm for European marathon and cycling traditions (Italian, Kenyan, Ethiopian schools). Formalized as a distinct intensity-distribution category in research by **Esteve-Lanao**, **Filipas** and colleagues from the late 2000s onward.

## Core principle

Three-tier pyramid where **volume decreases as intensity rises**: lots of easy work, a **meaningful chunk of tempo/threshold** (Zone 2/3 — between LT1 and LT2), and a small dose of high intensity above LT2. The grey zone is *not* avoided — it's a feature.

## Weekly intensity distribution

- **~70–75%** below LT1 (easy)
- **~20–25%** between LT1 and LT2 (tempo / threshold)
- **~5–10%** above LT2 (VO2 and above)

## Hallmark sessions

- **20–40 min steady tempo** @ ~85–90% LTHR / FTP.
- **2–3 × 15–20 min @ threshold** (~95–100% FTP / threshold pace).
- **Marathon-pace long run** with 60–90 min at goal effort.

## Periodization

Often used during base and build phases. Many athletes pyramidalize early and shift to polarized closer to peak (Filipas et al. 2022 showed the pyramidal → polarized switch maximized performance).

## Profile fit

Marathoners, long-course triathletes, sportive / gran-fondo cyclists training **8–15 h/week** who want sustainable, race-specific work without the volume cost of pure 80/20 or the strain of threshold-only plans.

## Anti-fit

Short-event athletes who need above-VO2max stimulus; athletes prone to "grey-zone" overreaching (chronic moderate-pace running); very low-volume athletes where the tempo bucket crowds out easy aerobic work.

## How this skill applies it

When designing workouts under this method:

1. Within a week aim for **one tempo/threshold session + one short VO2 or hill session + the rest easy**. The tempo day is the signature, not optional.
2. Tempo blocks: prefer `STEADY` 20–40 min @ **83–95% FTP / threshold pace / CSS**. Threshold reps: 2–3 × 15–20 min @ **95–100%**.
3. Keep VO2 work short — `3–5 × 3 min @ 110–115%` is enough.
4. Long sessions can include **race-pace segments** (e.g. 90min easy + 30min at marathon pace) — distinctive vs. polarized.
5. Label tempo blocks `"Z3 Tempo"` or `"Sweet Spot"`; threshold blocks `"Z4 Threshold"`.

## References

- Esteve-Lanao et al. 2017.
- Filipas et al. 2022 (*Scand J Med Sci Sports*) — 16-week pyramidal vs. polarized in well-trained runners.
- Renato Canova's marathon programs are widely cited as canonical pyramidal training.
